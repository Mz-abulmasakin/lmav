Who we are

A movement grounded
in people and purpose.

LMAV is a political support movement organised around the goal of APC victory. We aim to connect supporters, encourage constructive participation and help communities take part in public life.

Our guiding idea / 01

Strong communities create a stronger movement.

We believe that meaningful participation starts with listening, organisation and respect for the people in every community.

Our approach puts people at the centre: building relationships, sharing reliable information about the movement and creating practical ways for members and volunteers to contribute.

LMAV welcomes peaceful participation, teamwork and respectful dialogue. Our public activities and priorities should be communicated clearly and accurately to members and the wider community.
